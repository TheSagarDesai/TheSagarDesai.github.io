Privacy Policy

Happy Garden Kids Studio · Last updated October 26, 2026

Happy Garden Kids Studio ("the app") is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Happy Garden Kids to publish educational videos to that channel.

Data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests access to the owner's YouTube account in order to:

  • upload videos, captions and thumbnails to the owner's own channel;
  • read the owner's channel details and the status of uploaded videos;
  • read the owner's own YouTube Analytics reports for those videos.

The app does not access other users' accounts, contacts, email, or any data unrelated to the owner's channel.

How data is used and stored

  • Google access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation; they are never published or shared.
  • Data obtained through YouTube API Services is used only to publish and manage the owner's videos.
  • The app does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties and does not use it for advertising.
  • The app does not collect any information from viewers of the channel, including children.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. After revocation the stored tokens stop working; the owner can also delete them from the computer and the automation's secrets.
