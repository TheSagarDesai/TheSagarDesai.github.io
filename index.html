Happy Garden Kids Studio

A private tool that publishes educational cartoon videos for preschool children to the YouTube channel Happy Garden Kids.

What it does

The studio creates short learning episodes (counting, colors, shapes) with original characters, then uploads them to the channel owner's own YouTube channel, marked as Made for Kids, with titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails.

It is used only by the channel owner. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not access anyone else's YouTube data.

Google and YouTube access

The tool signs in with Google OAuth to upload videos, captions and thumbnails to the owner's channel and to read that channel's own statistics. See the Privacy Policy for details.
